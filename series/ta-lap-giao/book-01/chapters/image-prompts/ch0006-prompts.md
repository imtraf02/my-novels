# Prompts — Chương 6

## Prompt 1
> Cảnh này: Mở chương — cổng nhỏ Bạch Thảo Môn chỉ mở một khe, trước cổng đã có hàng dài người chờ từ khi trời chưa sáng hẳn.

Hand-drawn Chinese historical storybook illustration, simplified cute cartoon characters, old Chinese historical comic illustration.

FADED LOW-CONTRAST MONOCHROME SEPIA STYLE.

Use a very pale, desaturated sepia-brown color palette with soft beige paper tones.
The entire image should feel lightly faded and aged, like an old illustrated Chinese storybook printed with diluted brown ink on pale aged paper.

Keep the colors extremely subtle and low saturation.
Use only very soft variations of pale beige, warm gray-brown and faded sepia.
No vivid colors and no strong color accents.

The linework should be soft dark brown-gray rather than deep black.
Shadows should be gentle and translucent, never heavy or strongly contrasted.
Highlights should remain pale and close to the paper color.

Overall image should be light, washed-out, soft and low contrast.
The image should feel airy and slightly faded, not dark, not dramatic and not richly colored.

No blue, cyan, green, red, orange, purple or other noticeable colors.
No saturated colors.
No strong black outlines.
No high contrast.
No vivid lighting.

Characters are simplified, cute and slightly chubby cartoon figures with rounded heads and simplified bodies. They have very simple facial features, small dot eyes, tiny eyebrows and minimal facial lines. Their hands, arms, legs and bodies have simple cartoon shapes with visible volume, NOT thin stick limbs.

Traditional Chinese hairstyles, robes, hats, armor and accessories are clearly recognizable but drawn in a simple humorous cartoon style.

The characters should look like simple hand-drawn storybook characters. They must NOT look realistic, semi-realistic, anime, manga, or like detailed human portraits.

The environment is more detailed than the characters. Include traditional Chinese architecture, wooden structures, tiled roofs, palace halls, courtyards, furniture, trees, mountains, roads and everyday objects.

Use loose hand-drawn ink lines, slightly imperfect natural strokes, gentle cross-hatching and very subtle sketch shading.

Avoid polished digital vector art.
Avoid heavy outlines.
Avoid realistic rendering.
Avoid photorealism.
Avoid glossy coloring.
Avoid dramatic shadows.
Avoid strong contrast.

Cute, humorous, gentle historical storytelling atmosphere, visual clarity, layered foreground and background, cinematic 16:9 composition.

IMPORTANT:
The final image must be PALE, FADED, LOW-CONTRAST and DESATURATED.
It should resemble a lightly faded old Chinese storybook illustration.
Brown-gray ink on pale beige paper.
Very soft colors.
Very subtle shading.
No vivid colors.
No dark dramatic areas.

The characters must remain simplified cartoon people with minimal facial details and simple body shapes.
Do not make them realistic humans.
Do not make them thin stick figures.
Do not make them detailed historical people.
Do not make them anime characters.
Do not make realistic anatomy or realistic skin.

No text, no captions, no subtitles, no speech bubbles, no watermark, no modern objects.

Dawn light over a small mountain sect gate of a humble herb sect, the wooden gate opened only a narrow crack wide enough for two stacked palms. Outside the gate a long queue of more than ten villagers, farmers and merchants stands along a dirt path bordered by low stone markers and wild grass, some holding small blank wooden number tokens, some craning their necks toward the gate. Behind the crack a long polished plank counter is faintly visible. Misty mountain ridges rise behind tiled rooftops. Wide establishing shot, soft morning haze, queue receding into the distance.
